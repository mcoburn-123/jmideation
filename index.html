The Blend Board

Holding things up against the permaculture principles when we need clarity.

Start here

A planning board for a small team building a program that joins plants, people, water and learning. A first pass takes about 20 to 30 minutes. In the cards, “the lead” is the person who wants to build the program and “the partner” is the collaborator.

For the best results

  1. Pick the two or three cards that matter most to you. Drag each into Action Tracker, or use its Move menu on a phone.
  2. In the window that opens, click through every path before choosing. Choose the one closest to what you would really do.
  3. Answer the four best-practice questions honestly. “Not yet” and “Need to ask” are useful answers.
  4. Use Feedback & Notes for anything the board gets wrong: a missing person, an unrealistic path, wording that could cause harm, or a question that should be added.
  5. Tick off items in To Send and To Research as you go.
  6. Finish with Export Markdown, copy the text and send it back. Your work is saved only in this browser.

What to expect

  • Nine starting cards in four columns. They are drafts to react to, not recommendations.
  • Moving a card into Action Tracker opens three to five possible paths. Each has a three-step map: where it starts, what it becomes, and where it could lead.
  • Logging a path brings up four best-practice questions on consent, credit, giving back and starting small. You can skip them.
  • No account and no upload. Nothing leaves your device, and clearing your browser data erases your work, so export before you close the page.
  • Some entries stand in for real people and programs. Replace them in your notes as needed.
  • This is a thinking aid. It is not ethics approval, legal advice or medical advice.

How to use the board

1. Core Themes

The foundation: sense of belonging, water as the thread, the lead's past work and what the team hopes to build.

2. Structural & Anchors

The research that grounds the work, how the team could be organized, and the people who bridge to Brazil and to Indigenous medicine.

3. Action Tracker and outcome map

Drag any card here, or pick Action Tracker in its Move menu. A window opens with a few ways to go. Switching between them redraws a three-step map: where it starts, what it turns into, and where it could lead. Press "Log this path" and four best-practice questions pop up (consent, credit, giving back, starting small). Answer, or skip, and the path goes in the log. "Map outcomes" on a card in this column reopens it.

4. Notes and export

Pin notes in column 4. Export Markdown gathers the checklists, logged paths and notes into text you can copy and paste into a message or doc.
